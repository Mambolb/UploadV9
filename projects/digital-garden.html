Digital Garden

Link: https://youtu.be/S5CZSYIcKyY For this project I did interactive digital garden in Touchdesigner Description: What can spirituality look like in an environment of digitalization? Digital Garden explores this question through a spatial installation that invites visitors to interact and unwind as the garden grows. By moving a rainstick, a percussion instrument usually used for relaxation and meditation, visitors make a digital garden grow. The stick’s movement determines the garden’s growth and the swaying of its individual weeds, creating a visual metaphor of presence and spiritual growth. It weaves a connection between the rain-like sound of the stick, the visitor’s movement and the growth of the digital garden. Mariia piletska, Oleksandr Bichuk, Laura Hogrefe, Jonas Wenzel
