# Legal checklist before treating the site as a public professional portfolio

These are instructions, NOT a published legal notice. Do not use placeholder personal details in public pages.

## Impressum (§ 5 DDG)

For a German professional portfolio inviting commissions, a publicly accessible Impressum is strongly advised and likely legally required. Supply full legal name, a valid address at which legal correspondence can be served, and an email/contact channel allowing rapid direct communication. Additional details (e.g., business registers and VAT IDs) apply only if relevant. Do not use a fictional address, P.O. box, or your Instagram profile as a substitute.

## Datenschutzerklärung (GDPR Art. 13)

The statement must describe the actual practices, including: site operator and privacy contact, GitHub Pages as web host, GitHub access/IP logs, purpose/legal basis, recipients and third-country transfers, retention or criteria, data-subject rights and supervisory authority, email correspondence, and any external resources.

Current HTML loads Google Fonts from fonts.googleapis.com / fonts.gstatic.com. Visitors therefore contact Google servers. If avoiding this data transfer, replace external loading with suitably self-hosted font assets (requires licensing and local files). Do not claim zero external requests while Google Fonts remains.

## Cookie banner

A banner is not automatically necessary for a site with no non-essential cookies/trackers. Inspect third-party integrations (analytics, embeds, externally loaded assets) before deciding. Direct navigation links to Instagram/YouTube do not by themselves embed trackers into the page.

## Design integration

Add subtle IMPRINT and PRIVACY links to a site-wide footer or navigation, clearly and directly accessible on every page. Do this once the content and actual personal contact data are verified.
